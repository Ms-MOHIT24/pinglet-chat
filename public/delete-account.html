PINGLET · ACCOUNT CONTROL

Delete your account

Use this page if you have uninstalled Pinglet or cannot open the in-app deletion option. Sign in with your 14-character friend code and password, then confirm deletion.

Deletion permanently removes your account, friend connections, pending requests, and stored messages. This cannot be undone. Safety reports may be retained or de-identified when required for abuse prevention or legal obligations. A private key saved on a different device or browser cannot be remotely erased by this page.
