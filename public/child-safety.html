PINGLET · SAFETY FIRST

Child Safety Standards

Operator implementation draft · 7 November 2026

Pinglet does not tolerate child sexual abuse or exploitation. These standards describe prohibited conduct and the safeguards that the service operator must maintain before making the app publicly available.

Prohibited conduct

Users must not groom, sexually exploit, threaten, blackmail, solicit, sexualise, or otherwise harm a minor. Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), requests for it, links to it, trading, possession, or distribution are strictly prohibited. Do not download, forward, copy, or upload illegal material in order to report it.

In-app safety tools

Pinglet provides report controls on incoming messages and a report-account action, plus the ability to block a contact. A report can identify a concern such as sexual exploitation, harassment, threats, spam, or impersonation. Blocking removes the connection and prevents future friend requests and call-room connections between the accounts.

Review and response

Reports are stored in a protected moderation queue. This code does not, by itself, provide human moderation coverage, response-time guarantees, emergency escalation, or a staffed child-safety operation. The service operator must appoint trained reviewers, define escalation and preservation procedures, review reports promptly, document decisions, and comply with applicable reporting laws before launch.

Child-safety point of contact

NOT CONFIGURED — PUBLIC RELEASE BLOCKER. The responsible service operator must set and publish a monitored child-safety contact email address, and configure the matching service-side contact. Do not submit this draft to a store as proof of an active point of contact.

Cooperation and legal compliance

The service operator must maintain procedures for responding to valid legal requests, handling suspected CSAM without redistributing it, preserving only legally necessary evidence, and reporting to the appropriate authorities or designated reporting organisations where required by applicable law. Users should report concerns through the in-app controls and contact local emergency services if a person is in immediate danger.

Governance

Before release, the operator must document who owns child-safety decisions, who can access reports, how access is logged and limited, reviewer training, escalation paths, response targets, retention/deletion rules, and periodic policy reviews. These are operational requirements, not functions that a webpage alone can satisfy.

This is a draft policy template. It does not claim that Pinglet currently has a staffed moderation team or a configured child-safety contact.
