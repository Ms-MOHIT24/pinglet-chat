PINGLET · USER RULES

Terms of Use

Draft for product review · 7 November 2026

These Terms explain the rules for using Pinglet, a friend-connection and messaging service. By creating an account or continuing after the in-app terms prompt, you agree to follow these rules. The service operator must review and finalise these Terms before public release.

1. Eligibility and accounts

Use Pinglet only if you are legally permitted to use this kind of service where you live and meet the age requirements set by applicable law and the platform. Keep your friend code and password private. You are responsible for activity under your account and should tell the service operator about suspected unauthorised access.

2. Respectful and safe use

Do not use Pinglet to create, request, send, store, or distribute:

  • Harassment, bullying, stalking, intimidation, threats, or encouragement of violence.
  • Sexual exploitation, grooming, sexual harassment, or any sexual content involving a minor.
  • Child sexual abuse material or attempts to obtain, share, trade, or solicit it. Never upload or forward such material as part of a report.
  • Hate, discriminatory abuse, impersonation, scams, spam, or deceptive requests for money or private information.
  • Content that violates another person's privacy, intellectual-property rights, or applicable law.

Do not evade a block, repeatedly contact someone who does not want contact, misuse invite codes, or interfere with the service.

3. User content and messages

You remain responsible for the content you choose to send. Pinglet currently offers friend requests, one-to-one messages, short voice messages, games, and private call rooms. The current encryption design is experimental, has not been independently audited, and does not verify the identity of a contact's public key. Do not rely on it for sensitive or high-risk communications.

4. Reporting, blocking, and enforcement

You can report a message or account and block a contact from within the app. Reports are stored in a moderation queue. The service operator must review reports, apply appropriate actions, respond to serious safety concerns, and keep this process staffed. Blocking removes the friendship and prevents future friend requests or call-room connections between the blocked accounts. These controls do not replace contacting local emergency services where someone is in immediate danger.

5. Account deletion

You can request deletion inside Pinglet or through the public account deletion page. Deleting an account removes its user record, friendships, friend requests, and stored messages through database relationships. Safety reports may be retained or de-identified as needed for safety review and legal obligations.

6. Availability and changes

Pinglet is an early-stage service. Features may be unavailable, interrupted, or changed; call rooms and games can end when the server restarts. Do not use the service as your only copy of important information. The operator may restrict or suspend accounts that breach these Terms or create safety risks.

7. Contact and complaints

The service operator must publish a monitored support and safety contact address, a report-review process, and applicable legal/business details before public release. Those details are not yet configured in this draft; this page is not a substitute for them.

8. Privacy

How account and usage information is handled is explained in the Privacy Policy. By using Pinglet, you acknowledge that policy as well.

This is a product draft, not legal advice. The responsible service operator must obtain appropriate legal review and publish final, accurate terms before distribution.
