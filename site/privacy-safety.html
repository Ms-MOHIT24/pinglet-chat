PINGLET · TRANSPARENCY

Privacy & safety status

Public project information · 10 November 2026

This is an informational website

The Pinglet website is a static project-information page. It does not provide account registration, chat, calls, file uploads, or a contact form. Do not enter passwords, friend codes, private messages, or other personal information here.

The page uses locally hosted HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It does not include an analytics or advertising script. The website is hosted by Render; the hosting provider may process standard technical request information to operate and protect the site.

The app is not open for public access

Android downloads and public account access remain disabled while release checks are incomplete. A preview APK is not a production release and should not be used for real conversations.

App privacy and terms are still under review

The app's current Privacy Policy and Terms are technical drafts, not final legal documents. They need review by the responsible service operator and appropriate qualified reviewers. They must not be treated as final policies for a public service.

Safety and moderation are not yet launch-ready

The project has not yet verified a monitored safety contact or a staffed process for reviewing reports and responding to serious concerns. Those arrangements must be in place before public app access begins.

Website feedback

For non-sensitive website feedback, visit the Pinglet project repository. Do not post personal information, passwords, private conversations, access codes, or sensitive safety reports in public issues.

This page describes the current public website and project status. It is not a complete privacy notice for the Pinglet app and does not replace the final app policies required before app launch.
